# ESUT Campus Mapper — Field Scout Guide

Welcome to the **ESUT Agbani Campus Mapper**!  
We are building our own Google Maps-style navigator for ESUT Agbani—complete with every road, footpath, bush shortcut, and building entrance.

You don't need any technical experience to help. Just open the link on your phone and walk!

---

## 1. Before You Start Walking

1. **Open the `/mapper` link** on your phone browser (Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone).
2. **Enter your name** (e.g., *Munachi*, *Victor*) and pick the **Zone** you are starting in.
3. Tap **Start Mapping Session**.
4. **Allow Location & Camera permissions** when your phone asks.
5. **Check the GPS pill at the top:**
   - **Green (`±4m` to `±15m`)** = Good signal, ready to record!
   - **Red (`> 15m` or `No GPS`)** = Step out into the open air for a few seconds until it turns green.

> **Tip:** You can switch zones anytime by tapping your **Zone / Name** pill at the top-left. Tap **`?`** at the top-right anytime to see the map color guide.

---

## 2. What the Colors on the Map Mean

- **Blue dotted line** → Already mapped! Skip these so we don't map the same road twice.
- **Hollow blue ring** → Where someone stopped mapping. Walk there and tap it to continue the road seamlessly.
- **Pulsing orange dot** → An unmapped shortcut a teammate flagged. Tap it and hit **Claim & Guide** to let the map lead you there.
- **Green dashed line** → The path you are actively recording right now.
- **Blue dots** → Saved photos (building entrances, turns, and road views). Tap any dot or blue line to inspect or delete mistakes.

---

## 3. How to Map a Road or Footpath

1. **Stand at the start** of an unmapped road, junction, or hollow blue ring.
2. Tap **Start Path** (green button at the bottom).
3. **Walk normally** down the road—a green dashed line will trace behind you.
   - If you need to stop, talk, or enter a building, tap **Pause**. *(If you walk away while paused and tap Resume, the app will warn you so it doesn't draw a line through a building.)*
   - If your GPS glitches around a tall building, tap **Undo 3 pts** to erase the last few points.
   - If you accidentally walk onto an already-mapped blue road, an amber banner (**Already mapped**) will warn you so you can Pause or Discard.
4. **When you reach the end** of that road or a junction, tap **Save**.
5. Pick a quick name if you want (like **Main Road**, **Shortcut**, or **ETF Area**) and tap **Save Path**.
   - The road immediately turns **blue with dots** on everyone's phone!

---

## 4. When to Take Photos (**Snap**)

Photos make our campus navigator feel like Google Maps visual directions.

- **Every 50 meters (Road Views):**  
  Your phone will beep and the **Snap** button will turn green. Tap **Snap**, point your phone straight ahead down the road, take a quick photo, leave **Cadence View** selected, and tap **Save Pin**. *(It doesn't force you to stop—on long empty straight roads, you can snap every 50–80m.)*
- **At Every Major Building Entrance (Most Important!):**  
  Whenever you pass the entrance or signboard of a faculty, lecture hall, hostel, gate, library, medical center, or cafeteria:
  1. Tap **Snap** immediately (don't wait for 50m).
  2. Face the building so the photo and compass point directly at it.
  3. Select **Building Entrance**, tap or type the building's name (e.g., *ETF*, *Senate*, *SLT*, *Hostels*), and tap **Save Pin**.
- **At Confusing Turns / Shortcuts:**  
  Tap **Snap**, pick **Junction / Turn** or **Shortcut**, and save so students know where to turn.

---

## 5. Spotting a Side Shortcut (**Branch**)

Walking down a main road and spot a small footpath cutting off to the side that you don't want to walk right now?

1. Stand right at the entrance of that shortcut and tap **Branch**.
2. Tap a quick label (e.g., *Towards Hostels*, *Towards ETF*, *Side Gate*) and hit **Drop Pin & Continue Route**.
3. An **orange dot** pops up live on your teammates' maps so someone else can go map it—while you keep walking your current road uninterrupted!

---

## 6. Bad Network or Phone Reload? Don't Panic!

- **No 4G between lecture halls?** Keep walking and taking photos! Everything saves safely on your phone and uploads automatically the moment your network comes back.
- **Browser closed or refreshed mid-walk?** Re-open the link and tap **Recover Walk** to get your unsaved path right back.
